Read it like this

Certification's 203 items split three ways: reconciliation work and the manager's status views stay together, Journal Entries becomes its own area, and 129 setup screens go to Admin, split by which admin holds them.

What counts as setup

Configuration, rules, reference data, imports and security. In Match that is most of the menu, tuned by a few power users; moving it does not change their work.

Removed from customer navigation

The Disclosure Management and Workday tabs, Close Collaboration (zero page views) and the Legacy Consoles. Five items. On the board, every screen also carries its Pendo visitor tier.

The two moves that carry it

Admin follows the rights. Shared things get one home.

1 · One Admin tab, split by the three admin roles

System admins configure the site, user admins manage users and permissions, module admins configure their module. Admin mirrors that: User admin (Users, Templates, Access, as in the User Management prototype), System admin and Module admin. Managers do not get a tab of their own. The status views they open today under Management now sit inside each area: Reconciliations › Dashboard, Close › Status, Journal Entries › Dashboard, and the bulk queue under Reconciliations. The landing page links to them.

What changes for people

Each persona's question has one place to look.

Accountant
What remains, when is it due, and where do I act?
  • Home is the Florence landing page: favorites, my tasks, documents and helpful links, news.
  • The prepare, review and approve lists stay exactly as they are, inside Reconciliations.
  • Journal Entries is a top-level area instead of a side-menu item.
  • SmartMatch's two-grid layout and the dense tables are untouched; Matching keeps its tabs on the page.
Accounting manager
Where does work need attention, and who can move it forward?
  • The reconciliation, Close and journal entry status views sit inside each area; the landing page links to them.
  • Accounts, assignments and change requests sit with the module admin; a manager who holds that role finds them under Admin › Modules.
  • Reports in one tab.
Administrators
What must be set up, changed or resolved for work to continue?
  • User admins: Users, Templates and Access, as in the User Management prototype.
  • System admins: organization, calendars and periods, data and integrations, application, notices and documents.
  • Module admins: each module's configuration, plus its accounts, entities and tasks.

Kept on purpose

The role lists, the reconciliation record, SmartMatch, Close Home, and Compliance's internal structure. The assessment names these as strengths, so the redesign moves them rather than replacing them.

Help

Cadency Help and Beacon become one panel reachable from every screen. The help work itself is a separate initiative; the structure only gives it a place.

Persona questions are the dashboard research's own wording.
7 / 7
Decide, then validate

Two decisions for this room. Everything else can follow.

Decisions

  1. Which admin owns accounts, entities and tasks? Drawn under the module admin; the system admin, who holds calendars and periods, is the alternative. Brooke's dashboard research can settle it.
  2. Is QA review part of Reconciliations or its own area? QA review is the sample check an admin or QA reviewer runs on certified reconciliations.

Assumptions we are carrying

  1. Close shows only for customers who license it. A product question; some customers never open it.
  2. Periods can be managed in one place. Close uses periods, Certification uses effective dates and Match has neither, so one place needs a mapping layer between them. Possible, not free, and the prerequisite for cross-module dashboards.

The ask

Agree this structure as the basis for look-and-feel options and customer validation. Not a build decision.

What happens next

Look-and-feel options on the new structure. Thirty-minute sessions with accountants and accounting managers: can they find what needs attention and reach the item or person they need? The first screens built on it are the Florence shell (the new app frame) and its landing page.
